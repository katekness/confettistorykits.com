Play safely

The kits are designed for children ages 4 to 8 with an adult in charge. Please supervise play and choose safe hiding spots: avoid stoves, ovens, high shelves, sharp or breakable items, cleaning supplies, and anywhere a child might climb or get stuck. Small treats or prizes can be choking hazards for young children.
