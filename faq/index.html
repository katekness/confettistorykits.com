Frequently asked questions

What is a Confetti Story Kit?

A printable treasure hunt held together by a story. You print the clue cards, hide them around the house, and kids follow rhyming riddles from one hiding spot to the next until they find the treasure at the end.

What is the Quest Master?

A web page that runs in your phone's browser. Scan the QR code on your printed kit and it tells the story with your child's name in it, reads each clue out loud, gives hints when someone gets stuck, and throws a confetti celebration at the finish. There is no app to download, no account and no ads.

What ages are the kits for?

Ages 4 to 8. Younger kids can play with a grown-up reading the clues; older kids can read and race on their own.

How long does setup take?

About 5 minutes. Print, cut, and hide the cards using the hiding spot checklist included in every kit.

Do I need a printer?

Yes, the kits are a PDF you print at home on regular paper or cardstock, US Letter size (8.5 x 11 in). Nothing is shipped.

Can I use my own hiding spots?

Yes. Every kit includes blank clue cards. Write your own riddle, then type it into the Quest Master under Host setup so the phone reads your version.

Do the printed clues work without a phone?

Yes. The Quest Master is a bonus; every clue card works on its own.

Where do I buy the kits?

Kits are sold as instant digital downloads in the Confetti Story Kits shop on Etsy.

Can I print a kit more than once?

Yes, print it as often as you like for your own family's celebrations. Please don't share or resell the file.
