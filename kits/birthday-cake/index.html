The Great Birthday Cake Quest

The birthday cake has vanished. Follow the clues to find it!

A printable birthday adventure where kids follow clues to discover the hidden cake. Your phone becomes the Quest Master: The Princess and The Knight read each clue out loud, give hints when someone is stuck, and celebrate with confetti at the end.

What's included

  • Magical story introduction
  • 10 ready-to-use clue cards
  • Quest Master on your phone: reads the clues, gives hints, throws the confetti
  • Blank cards for your own hiding spots
  • Explorer certificate
  • Setup guide and hiding spot checklist
  • Party tips

Where the clues hide

Every room is a stop on the trail. The checklist covers spots like:

  • Shoe area or entryway
  • Inside or near the refrigerator
  • TV, couch or movie area
  • Bathroom sink
  • Toy area or playroom
  • A bed or pillow
  • Kitchen table or utensil drawer
  • Washer or laundry area
  • Books or a bookshelf
  • Near the birthday cake

No laundry room? Use a blank card and type your own clue into the Quest Master.

How it works

  1. Print and cut the cards (about 5 minutes).
  2. Hide them using the checklist. Clue #1 stays in your hand.
  3. Scan the code on the kit, add your child's name, and start the adventure.

Perfect for birthday parties, birthday morning surprises, playdates, rainy days.
