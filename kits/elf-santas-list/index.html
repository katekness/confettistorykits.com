The Elf Who Lost Santa's List

A North Pole wind scattered Santa's list. Find every page before Christmas Eve!

A printable Christmas page hunt where kids find the pieces of Santa's list before Christmas Eve. Your phone becomes the Quest Master: it tells the story, reads each clue out loud, gives hints when someone is stuck, and celebrates with confetti at the end.

What's included

  • Letter from Juniper the Elf
  • 10 clue cards styled as Santa's torn list pages
  • Quest Master on your phone: reads the clues, gives hints, throws the confetti
  • Blank pages for your own hiding spots
  • North Pole Helper certificate
  • Setup guide and hiding spot checklist
  • Party tips

Where the clues hide

Every room is a stop on the trail. The checklist covers spots like:

  • Inside or near the refrigerator
  • Stockings or fireplace (or a window ledge)
  • Bathroom sink
  • Couch cushions
  • Books or a bookshelf
  • A bed or pillow
  • Shoes or boots by the door
  • Toy box or playroom
  • Kitchen utensil drawer or cookie jar
  • Under the Christmas tree

No laundry room? Use a blank card and type your own clue into the Quest Master.

How it works

  1. Print and cut the cards (about 5 minutes).
  2. Hide them using the checklist. Clue #1 stays in your hand.
  3. Scan the code on the kit, add your child's name, and start the adventure.

Perfect for Christmas Eve, winter break, holiday parties, family Christmas mornings.

Great for groups, too

  • Classrooms: hide the cards around the room or school and read clues together. Builds teamwork, close reading and problem solving.
  • Homeschool and co-ops: a story-led activity that practices reading, rhyming and logic.
  • Scouts, clubs and after-school programs: split into teams and race to the treasure.
  • Libraries and kids' ministry: an easy add to story time, family nights or holiday events.

Swap any hiding spot for one in your space with the blank clue cards. One copy per teacher or leader.
