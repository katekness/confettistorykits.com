The Case of the Missing Treasure

The treasure box vanished from Detective Headquarters. Crack the case!

A printable detective mystery where kids follow clues to find the missing treasure. Your phone becomes the Quest Master: Detective Ivy and Detective Theo read each clue out loud, give hints when someone is stuck, and celebrate with confetti at the end.

What's included

  • Detective story case file
  • 10 rhyming clue cards
  • Quest Master on your phone: reads the clues, gives hints, throws the confetti

  • Junior Detective certificate
  • Setup guide and hiding spot checklist

Where the clues hide

Every room is a stop on the trail. The checklist covers spots like:

  • Inside or near the refrigerator
  • Bathroom sink
  • Couch cushions
  • Books or a bookshelf
  • Shoe area or entryway
  • A bed or pillow
  • Toy box or playroom
  • Washer or laundry area
  • Coat closet
  • Under the kitchen or dining table

No laundry room? Use a blank card and type your own clue into the Quest Master.

How it works

  1. Print and cut the cards (about 5 minutes).
  2. Hide them using the checklist. Clue #1 stays in your hand.
  3. Scan the code on the kit, add your child's name, and start the adventure.

Perfect for detective and spy parties, birthdays, playdates, sleepovers, rainy days.
