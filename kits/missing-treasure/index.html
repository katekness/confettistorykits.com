The Case of the Missing Treasure

The treasure box vanished from Detective Headquarters. Crack the case!

A printable detective mystery where kids follow clues to find the missing treasure. Your phone becomes the Quest Master: it tells the story, reads each clue out loud, gives hints when someone is stuck, and celebrates with confetti at the end.

  • Detective story case file

Where the clues hide

Every room is a stop on the trail. The checklist covers spots like:

  • Inside or near the refrigerator
  • Bathroom sink
  • Couch cushions
  • Books or a bookshelf
  • Shoe area or entryway
  • A bed or pillow
  • Toy box or playroom
  • Washer or laundry area
  • Coat closet
  • Under the kitchen or dining table

No laundry room? Use a blank card and type your own clue into the Quest Master.

How it works

  1. Print and cut the cards (about 5 minutes).
  2. Hide them using the checklist. Clue #1 stays in your hand.
  3. Scan the code on the kit, add your child's name, and start the adventure.

Perfect for detective and spy parties, birthdays, playdates, sleepovers, rainy days.

Great for groups, too

  • Classrooms: hide the cards around the room or school and read clues together. Builds teamwork, close reading and problem solving.
  • Homeschool and co-ops: a story-led activity that practices reading, rhyming and logic.
  • Scouts, clubs and after-school programs: split into teams and race to the treasure.
  • Libraries and kids' ministry: an easy add to story time, family nights or holiday events.

Swap any hiding spot for one in your space with the blank clue cards. One copy per teacher or leader.
