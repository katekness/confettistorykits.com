Where crafting meets storytelling

Printable story adventures you set up in minutes. Print the clues and hide them around the house. Your phone becomes the Quest Master: it tells the story, reads each riddle, gives hints when someone's stuck, and throws the confetti at the finish.

How it works

1

Print & hide

Print the clue cards and tuck them around the house using the hiding spot guide.

2

Scan to start

Every kit has a QR code that opens its Quest Master. Add your kid's name and the story is about them now.

3

Let them hunt

The phone reads each clue out loud. Kids run, search, shout, and find the treasure.
