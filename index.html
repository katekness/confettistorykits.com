How it works

1

Print & hide

Print the clue cards at home and tuck them around the house using the hiding spot checklist.

2

Scan to start

Every kit has a QR code that opens its Quest Master. Add your kid's name and the story is about them now.

3

Let them hunt

The phone reads each clue out loud. Kids run, search, shout, and find the treasure.

Why parents like them

Screen-light

The phone stays with the grown-up. Kids spend the whole time running around the house, not staring at a screen.

Their name in the story

Type your child's name into the Quest Master and the characters talk to them directly.

Works in any home

Blank clue cards let you swap any hiding spot, and the phone reads your version.
