How it works

1

Print & hide

Print the cards at home and tuck them around the house using the hiding spot checklist. About five minutes.

2

Scan to start

Scan the QR code on your kit. Add your kid's name and the story is about them now.

3

Let them hunt

The phone reads each clue out loud. Kids run, search, shout, and find the treasure.
